How to read this capture
    

    
Roles and terms come from Raft log messages. Health comes from “group health changed” observations; it is not a fresh quorum proof. Missing observations stay unknown. Lines show cluster relationships, not measured traffic or verified connectivity.

    
Striped intervals use the test’s logged isolation schedule. The interval end is a scheduled repair, not a logged acknowledgement. Logs without explicit partition events cannot show their network faults. Quorum totals summarize the entire test, not the current frame.

    

    

      
Docker captures sample existing local runtime and cluster-status endpoints every 500 ms, with a 400 ms timeout per request. Slow sweeps skip ticks. The diagram shows metadata leadership: “nonleader” does not distinguish a follower, learner or candidate. Failed runtime reads clear the displayed role to unknown and mark the endpoint unreachable; they do not prove that the process is dead.

      
Startup-ready is the Node startup gate, not current mutation readiness or quorum proof. Observation detail contains session liveness, local load lifecycle, topology versions, group observations and their original timestamps. Each node reports its own view; views can disagree and are not an atomic cluster snapshot. State between samples remains the last observation. No fault or connectivity schedule is inferred.
